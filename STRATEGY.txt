--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. WHAT THIS SYSTEM IS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

This is a live and backtest algorithmic trading system built on NautilusTrader
(v1.231.0) that connects to a MetaTrader5 terminal (FxPro demo account) and
trades multiple instruments automatically using an EMA crossover strategy.

Instruments traded (live):
  GOLD, #USNDAQ100, #USSPX500, WTI, BITCOIN, #Japan225

Account: FxPro demo, JPY-denominated, retail hedging mode.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. HOW THE STRATEGY WORKS (SIMPLE EXPLANATION)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The strategy uses two Exponential Moving Averages (EMAs) — a fast one and a
slow one — to decide when to buy and when to exit.

  ENTRY (BUY):
    When the fast EMA crosses ABOVE the slow EMA (called a "golden cross"),
    the strategy opens a BUY (long) position. This means the price is starting
    to trend upward.

  EXIT (close the trade):
    The trade can be closed in three ways:
      1. TP hit     — price reaches the Take Profit level (profit target)
      2. SL hit     — price drops to the Stop Loss level (loss limit)
      3. Death cross — fast EMA crosses BELOW slow EMA (trend reversal signal)

The strategy ONLY trades long (buys). It never goes short.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. ENTRY SIGNAL IN DETAIL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Every bar (candle), the strategy checks:

  Was fast EMA below slow EMA on the PREVIOUS bar?
  AND is fast EMA now ABOVE slow EMA on THIS bar?
  → YES: Golden cross detected → open a BUY

  Was fast EMA above slow EMA on the PREVIOUS bar?
  AND is fast EMA now BELOW slow EMA on THIS bar?
  → YES: Death cross detected → close all open longs for that instrument

The EMAs are calculated from the bar close prices. The strategy waits for
enough historical bars to "warm up" before it can trade (warmup_bars = 200).

Configuration:
  fast_ema_period = 20   (20-bar EMA, reacts faster to price changes)
  slow_ema_period = 50   (50-bar EMA, smoother long-term trend)
  bar_spec (live)        = "15-MINUTE-LAST-EXTERNAL"  (15-minute bars)
  bar_spec (backtest)    = "1-HOUR-LAST-EXTERNAL"     (1-hour bars)


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. STOP LOSS AND TAKE PROFIT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The SL and TP are set automatically based on the ATR (Average True Range).
ATR measures how much the price typically moves in one bar — a bigger ATR
means more volatility, so the SL/TP are wider to avoid being stopped out by
normal noise.

  ATR period = 14 bars

  Initial SL = entry price  -  (sl_atr_mult × ATR)
  Initial TP = entry price  +  (tp_atr_mult × ATR)

Live settings:
  sl_atr_mult = 1.5   →   SL is 1.5 × ATR below entry
  tp_atr_mult = 3.0   →   TP is 3.0 × ATR above entry
  Risk-reward ratio  = 3.0 / 1.5 = 2:1

Backtest settings:
  sl_atr_mult = 2.0
  tp_atr_mult = 4.0
  Risk-reward ratio  = 4.0 / 2.0 = 2:1

Example (GOLD, ATR = 10.00, entry at 4000.00):
  SL = 4000.00 - (1.5 × 10.00) = 3985.00
  TP = 4000.00 + (3.0 × 10.00) = 4030.00

Every bar, the strategy checks:
  bar.high >= TP  →  close position (take profit)
  bar.low  <= SL  →  close position (stop loss)


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. TRAILING STOP (PROTECTING PROFITS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

This is the key money-management feature. Once a trade moves into profit,
the stop loss is automatically moved up so you can NEVER lose money on it.

STEP 1 — Move to Breakeven:
  Once the profit reaches breakeven_r × initial_risk (default = 1.0 × risk),
  the SL is moved UP to the entry price. At this point, the worst outcome
  is breaking even — you cannot make a loss on this trade.

  Example: entry = 4000, SL = 3985, risk = 15 points
  Once price reaches 4015 (profit = 15 = 1 × risk):
  → SL moves from 3985 up to 4000 (entry = breakeven)

STEP 2 — Trail the Stop:
  After breakeven, as price continues rising, the SL keeps following it.
  The SL always stays one "risk distance" below the current close price.

  Example continuing from above:
  Price rises to 4020 → SL trails to 4020 - 15 = 4005
  Price rises to 4025 → SL trails to 4025 - 15 = 4010
  If price then drops, SL stays at 4010 — it never moves down.

The result: the longer the trade runs in profit, the more profit is locked in.
The trade can only be stopped out with a profit once SL > entry.

Configuration:
  breakeven_r = 1.0   (move SL to entry after 1R profit)
  trail_sl    = True  (keep trailing after breakeven)


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. POSITION LIMITS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The strategy enforces two limits to control total exposure:

  max_per_instrument = 2    max open positions per symbol at any time
  max_total          = 10   max open positions across ALL symbols combined

If a golden cross fires but a limit is already reached, the signal is skipped.
The strategy also counts PENDING orders (submitted but not yet filled) toward
the limit, to prevent a burst of fills after multiple quick crosses.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. ENTER ON TREND (LIVE ONLY)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

On live trading, enter_on_trend = True.

When the strategy starts up after the warmup period, if the fast EMA is
already above the slow EMA (price is already trending up), it pretends a
golden cross just happened and opens a trade immediately. Without this,
the strategy would wait for the next actual cross, which could take hours
or days while the trend is already running.

On backtest, enter_on_trend = False (avoids false signals at history start).


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  run_live.py
    └── TradingNode (NautilusTrader)
          ├── MT5DataClient         (receives price data from MT5)
          │     └── worker thread: polls MT5 terminal every 0.25s
          │           ├── _poll_bars()   — sends new completed bars to strategy
          │           └── _poll_ticks()  — sends quote ticks (bid/ask)
          │
          ├── MT5ExecutionClient    (sends orders to MT5)
          │     └── worker thread: processes order queue
          │           └── _send_order()  — calls mt5.order_send()
          │
          └── EMACross (strategy)
                ├── on_bar()             — main logic each bar
                ├── _manage_positions()  — SL/TP/trailing stop check
                ├── _open_long()         — submit BUY market order
                ├── _close_all()         — submit SELL for all open positions
                ├── on_position_opened() — record entry price, set SL/TP levels
                └── on_position_closed() — clean up tracking state

Threading model:
  - All MT5 calls happen in a single daemon worker thread (MT5 is not
    thread-safe). The asyncio event loop never touches mt5.* directly.
  - Data flows: worker → call_soon_threadsafe → event loop → strategy.
  - Orders flow: event loop → queue → worker → mt5.order_send().


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
9. HOW ORDERS ARE PLACED ON MT5
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

When the strategy calls close_position() or submit_order(), NautilusTrader
routes the command to MT5ExecutionClient._submit_order().

For OPENING a position:
  - A BUY MARKET order is sent via mt5.order_send()
  - MT5 returns a position ticket (e.g. 352101107)
  - This ticket becomes the NautilusTrader PositionId
  - The strategy's on_position_opened() fires and records SL/TP levels

For CLOSING a position:
  - A SELL MARKET order is sent with position=<MT5 ticket>
  - In FxPro hedging mode, specifying position= closes THAT specific trade
  - Without position=, MT5 would open a new short instead of closing

The MT5 ticket is stored as the NautilusTrader PositionId directly, so
when a close is needed, command.position_id.value is already the ticket.

10. BACKTEST
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

File: run_backtest.py
Data source: H1 (1-hour) bars from MT5, stored in a Parquet catalog.

To run:
  .venv\Scripts\python pull_h1.py          # download H1 bars from MT5
  .venv\Scripts\python build_catalog.py    # convert to Parquet catalog
  .venv\Scripts\python run_backtest.py     # run the backtest

The backtest saves a checkpoint after each run (.last_run_ts). Subsequent
runs only process new bars since the last checkpoint (plus a warmup window).
Use --full flag to force a complete re-run from all history.

11. CONFIGURATION REFERENCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

EMACrossConfig parameters:

  instruments        List of instrument IDs to trade.
                     e.g. ("GOLD.FXPRO", "#USNDAQ100.FXPRO")

  bar_spec           Bar type string. Live: "15-MINUTE-LAST-EXTERNAL"
                     Backtest: "1-HOUR-LAST-EXTERNAL"

  fast_ema_period    Period for the fast EMA. Default: 20
  slow_ema_period    Period for the slow EMA. Default: 50
  atr_period         Period for ATR (volatility). Default: 14

  trade_sizes        Lot size per instrument.
                     e.g. {"GOLD.FXPRO": "0.01"}

  warmup_bars        How many bars to load before trading starts. Default: 200
                     (needs enough to initialise slow EMA + ATR)

  max_per_instrument Max simultaneous positions per symbol. Default: 2
  max_total          Max simultaneous positions total. Default: 10

  enter_on_trend     If True, enter immediately after warmup if already
                     trending. Default: False (True for live)

  sl_atr_mult        Stop loss distance = this × ATR. Default: 2.0
                     Set to 0.0 to disable SL.

  tp_atr_mult        Take profit distance = this × ATR. Default: 4.0
                     Set to 0.0 to disable TP.
                     For 2:1 ratio: tp_atr_mult = 2 × sl_atr_mult

  breakeven_r        Move SL to entry after this many R in profit.
                     Default: 1.0 (move to breakeven after 1R profit)
                     Set to 0.0 to disable.

  trail_sl           After breakeven, trail SL behind price. Default: True

13. HOW TO RUN LIVE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Prerequisites:
  1. MetaTrader5 terminal open, logged into FxPro demo
  2. Tools → Options → Expert Advisors → "Allow Algo Trading" checked
  3. .venv activated

Create .env file with credentials:
  MT5_LOGIN=12345678
  MT5_PASSWORD=yourpassword
  MT5_SERVER=FxPro-Demo

Run:
  .venv\Scripts\python run_live.py

Stop:
  Ctrl+C  — the strategy will close all open positions on shutdown
