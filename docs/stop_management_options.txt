===============================================================================
 STOP / TAKE-PROFIT MANAGEMENT — DESIGN OPTIONS & DECISION
 newgold — EMACross strategy + MT5 (FxPro demo)
 Written 2026-10-07
===============================================================================

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 THE PROBLEM (why this doc exists)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Right now the exit of a position is managed in TWO places at once, and they
do not agree on the price levels:

  1. BROKER-SIDE (MT5)
     - strategy/ema_cross.py _open_long() attaches SL:/TP: tags to the entry
       order. adapters/mt5/execution.py reads those tags and sends a real
       stop-loss + take-profit to MT5 (request["sl"], request["tp"]).
     - These levels are computed from the SIGNAL-BAR close and are STATIC:
       they are set once at entry and never updated.

  2. STRATEGY-SIDE (in-process)
     - strategy/ema_cross.py _manage_positions() runs every bar close:
       checks SL/TP hit, moves SL to break-even, then trails the SL.
     - These levels are computed from the FILL-TIME ATR and MOVE over the
       life of the trade. They are NOT mirrored back to the broker.

Because both exist with different numbers, whichever fires first wins and the
other becomes stale. Observed live on WTI (2026-10-07):

     entry        89.26
     broker TP    90.260  (static tag)
     internal TP  90.33   (fill-time)
     trailed SL   89.69   (strategy, after break-even)

   -> Price spiked; broker TP (90.260) fired at the venue, closing at 90.261.
   -> One bar later the strategy's trail check saw low=89.59, thought "SL HIT",
      and submitted a close. The venue returned retcode 10013 (already closed);
      the adapter gracefully synthesized the close fill. Net: small profit kept.
   -> BUT the trade was logged as reason=SL when it actually closed at TP.

Two distinct issues come out of this:
   (A) Mislabeled close reason in the trade history.   <-- FIXED (see below)
   (B) The trailing stop is partly fiction: the broker still holds the ORIGINAL
       wide SL, so a hard intrabar drop stops you out at the old level, not the
       trailed one. This is the design decision still to make.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 WHAT WAS ALREADY FIXED (2026-10-07)  — issue (A)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
monitor_actor.py now reconciles the close reason against the ACTUAL exit price
instead of trusting the strategy's pre-close guess. See _reconcile_reason():
a price-level exit ("TP"/"SL") is re-attributed to whichever level the fill
landed closest to; event-driven reasons (EMA, SHUTDOWN, CLOSED) are trusted
as-is. The WTI case above now logs reason=TP correctly.

This is a reporting fix only. It does NOT change how trades are entered or
exited — issue (B) below is still open.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 THE DECISION — WHO OWNS THE STOP?
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

OPTION A — STRATEGY OWNS THE STOP
---------------------------------
  What changes:
    - Stop sending SL:/TP: tags on the entry order (drop them in _open_long).
    - All exits handled by _manage_positions() on bar close, as it already does.
  Pros:
    - Single source of truth. No divergence, no 10013 reconciliation races.
    - Break-even + trailing already implemented and working.
    - No changes to the hand-written MT5 adapter.
  Cons:
    - Exits only evaluated on BAR CLOSE (15-min bars currently). No intrabar
      protection: a sharp move between closes is unhedged until the next bar.
    - If the bot process dies, there is NO stop at the broker at all — the
      position sits naked until the bot restarts.
  Effort: small (remove tag construction, maybe a config flag).

OPTION B — BROKER OWNS THE STOP
-------------------------------
  What changes:
    - Drop _manage_positions() internal SL/TP/trailing.
    - Build an ORDER-MODIFY path in adapters/mt5/execution.py so that when the
      strategy wants to move the stop (break-even / trail), it updates the
      broker-side SL/TP on the live position.
  Pros:
    - Real intrabar protection at the venue (ticks, not bar closes).
    - Stop survives a bot/process crash.
  Cons:
    - Requires new hand-written MT5 adapter code (position modify / SL-TP
      update). Per CLAUDE.md the adapter is written by hand and must NOT be
      auto-generated — this is work for the user, not for Claude.
    - Trailing cadence limited by how often the strategy pushes updates.
  Effort: medium (new adapter capability + wiring + testing).

OPTION C — HYBRID (broker = disaster stop, strategy = active management)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
  What changes:
    - Keep a STATIC, WIDE broker-side SL as a catastrophe backstop only
      (e.g. well beyond the strategy SL). Optionally a static broker TP.
    - Strategy continues break-even + trailing and normally closes via market
      BEFORE the wide broker stop is ever reached.
  Pros:
    - Best of both: intrabar + crash protection from the broker backstop,
      active trailing from the strategy.
    - They no longer "disagree" in practice because the broker level is set
      far away and is only a safety net.
  Cons:
    - Two mechanisms still exist; needs care that the backstop is genuinely
      wider than anything the strategy will do, so the strategy always fires
      first in normal conditions.
    - Still needs the reconciliation fix (already done) for the rare backstop
      hit.
  Effort: small-to-medium (set wide tags; no adapter modify strictly required
    if the backstop stays static).

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 RECOMMENDATION
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Near term: OPTION A (strategy owns the stop).
  - It removes the divergence immediately, needs no adapter work (respecting the
    CLAUDE.md rule that MT5 adapter code is hand-written), and the trailing logic
    already exists and works. Accept the bar-close-only limitation for now — on
    a demo account with 15-min bars the exposure window is bounded and the point
    right now is to validate the strategy, not to harden execution.

Longer term: move to OPTION C (hybrid).
  - Once you want crash/intrabar safety, add a WIDE static broker SL as a pure
    backstop. This gives protection without reintroducing the level-fight,
    because the strategy's trail is always tighter and fires first.
  - OPTION B (broker fully owns, with live modify) is only worth the adapter
    work if you later want the trail itself to live at the venue tick-by-tick.

Whatever is chosen, the close-reason reconciliation already added to
monitor_actor.py stays — it is correct under all three options.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 KNOWN RELATED ISSUE (not fixed, noted for later)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
rr_ratio in monitor_actor._on_position_closed mixes units: profit is in account
/quote currency (e.g. USD) while risk is in PRICE points (entry - sl). The
resulting rr is not a true reward/risk multiple. Fix separately by expressing
both in the same unit (e.g. price move achieved / initial price risk, or PnL /
risk-in-PnL-terms).
