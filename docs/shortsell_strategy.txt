===============================================================================
 SHORT-SELL STRATEGY  —  EMA crossover (mirror of the live long-only system)
 newgold / NautilusTrader 1.231.0 / MT5 FxPro demo
===============================================================================

PURPOSE
  The live EMACross strategy is long-only: it enters on a golden cross
  (fast EMA crossing above slow) and never shorts. This document specs the
  short side so it can run as a mirror — either standalone or as the opposite
  leg of a long/short strategy on the same 6 instruments.

  FxPro is retail-hedging (OmsType.HEDGING), so a short can be opened
  independently of any long on the same symbol. No netting conflict.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 1. INSTRUMENTS & TIMEFRAME  (unchanged from the long system)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Symbols : GOLD, #USNDAQ100, #USSPX500, WTI, BITCOIN, #Japan225
  Venue   : FXPRO
  Bars    : 15-MINUTE, LAST, EXTERNAL
  Account : JPY (positions report USD PnL; broker converts to JPY)


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 2. INDICATORS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  fast = ExponentialMovingAverage(20)
  slow = ExponentialMovingAverage(50)
  atr  = AverageTrueRange(14, SIMPLE)

  Warm up all three from a 200-bar history request before trading (same as
  the long side). Only act on CLOSED bars.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 3. ENTRY — SHORT (the mirror of the golden cross)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Signal: DEATH CROSS
    prev bar:  fast >= slow
    this bar:  fast <  slow      --> open SHORT on bar close

  enter_on_trend (optional, mirrors live behaviour, OFF by default):
    If fast < slow already at warmup, treat it as a fresh death cross and
    enter short on the next bar.
    CAUTION: the live long side does this and it opened a mid-trend WTI
    position on restart that got stopped out. If you enable it on the short
    side, expect the same — entering an already-extended downtrend. Prefer
    OFF for shorts unless you specifically want trend-continuation entries.

  Filters to consider (optional):
    - skip if atr == 0 or history incomplete
    - respect max positions per instrument and global max (see section 6)


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 4. STOPS & TARGETS  (mirror of the long ATR bracket — flip the sign)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Long side used, per the live logs:
      SL = entry - 1.5 * ATR
      TP = entry + 3.0 * ATR        (≈ 1 : 2 risk/reward)

  Short side (mirror):
      entry = bar close (fill price used for the actual bracket)
      SL    = entry + 1.5 * ATR     (stop ABOVE price)
      TP    = entry - 3.0 * ATR     (target BELOW price)

  Software-managed exits (same as live — these are NOT broker-side orders,
  they are evaluated each bar and closed with a reduce-only MARKET order):

    SL HIT (short):      bar HIGH >= SL          -> close
    TP HIT (short):      bar LOW  <= TP          -> close
    SL -> BREAKEVEN:     once profit >= 1.0 * ATR, move SL down to entry
    SL TRAIL (short):    trail SL downward to   close + 1.5 * ATR
                         (never move it up; trail only tightens)

  Rounding: round SL/TP to each instrument's price_precision
            (GOLD = 2 dp / point 0.01). Do not send more precision than the
            symbol allows or the order is rejected.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 5. POSITION SIZING  (fix the imbalance seen in the long logs)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Hardcoded lots risk wildly different JPY per instrument. Yesterday's live
  session: WTI 0.1 lot risked ~8,500 JPY (~5% of a 177k account) while GOLD
  0.01 lot risked ~580 JPY. Size off money-at-risk instead:

      qty_lots = risk_JPY / (SL_distance * contract_size * usdjpy)

    where
      SL_distance   = 1.5 * ATR   (price units)
      contract_size = mt5.symbol_info(sym).trade_contract_size
                      (e.g. GOLD = 100 oz/lot, WTI = 1000 bbl/lot — VERIFY
                       per symbol, do not assume)
      usdjpy        = current USDJPY (positions are USD-denominated)
      risk_JPY      = fixed per-trade budget, e.g. 0.5% of equity

    Then clamp to the symbol's volume_min / volume_step / volume_max.

  Current fixed WTI size is 0.02 lot (reduced from 0.1) — fine as an interim
  cap, but the formula above is the real fix.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 6. RISK LIMITS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  max per instrument : 2 concurrent positions   (as in live logs: "pos 1/2")
  max total          : 10 concurrent positions   ("total 1/10")
  one entry per bar per instrument


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 7. SHORT-SPECIFIC CAVEATS  (do NOT just mirror and forget these)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - SWAP / CARRY: short swap can be negative on indices/metals held overnight.
    Check mt5.symbol_info(sym).swap_short before running overnight. Shorts are
    not symmetric with longs on financing.
  - GAP RISK: software stops are evaluated on bar data; a gap through your SL
    (weekend/news) fills worse than SL. More dangerous on the short side of
    equity indices (melt-up gaps) and crypto (24/7, violent squeezes).
  - SHORT SQUEEZE: trends up can be faster than trends down. The 1.5 ATR stop
    that was comfortable long may be too tight short — consider 2.0 ATR.
  - BITCOIN trades 24/7; the others have sessions. A death cross at an illiquid
    hour can slip badly on the short entry fill.
  - Verify the symbol is shortable on the demo and margin is available before
    assuming the order fills (watch for retcode 10013 / invalid request, which
    the exec client already handles by synthesising the close fill).


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 8. IMPLEMENTATION NOTES (when you wire it into the Nautilus strategy)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Read the installed Nautilus API at
    .venv/Lib/site-packages/nautilus_trader/  — do not write calls from memory.
  - OMS: HEDGING (short can coexist with a long on the same symbol).
  - Order: MarketOrder SELL to open; reduce_only MARKET BUY to close.
  - Tag the entry with SL/TP for the dashboard, e.g. tags=['SL:xxxx','TP:xxxx'],
    exactly as the long side does.
  - MonitorActor already tracks opened/closed with reason + rr; the short close
    reason will be SL / TP / TRAIL just like the long side.
  - Backtest the short side on M15 history (M5/H1/D1 back to 2008, M1 to ~2020)
    before going live on the demo. Shorts behave differently across the 2020
    and 2022 regimes — test both.
